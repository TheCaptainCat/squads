## Scope

A role or operator body may hold prose. `sq check` accepts it, `sq role <slug> body` and
`sq operator <slug> body` set it, and the `greeting` skill reads an operator's body as their
profile and interviews a human it does not know. Covers FEAT-1054 US1 to US4.

Out of scope (the feature's own list): a profile frontmatter key, a `--profile` option on
`operator add`, a profile schema, interview behaviour for agent roles, any change to the
system-entry refusal, and skill bodies. The skill branch of the "missing seeded view tag ...
already carries prose" report is unchanged: the ruling names agent roles and operators.

## Rulings this task carries (binding, not reopened)

- Prose is allowed in role and operator bodies; `sq check` stops reporting it (REV-1047 F8,
  op-pierre, 2026-10-16).
- The `body` verb is for roles and operators; the profile is the item's body as prose; no
  `--profile` on `operator add`; the feature targets 0.16 (op-pierre, 2026-10-16, FEAT-1054).
- `sq role <slug> body` is writable exactly where a role body is writable today: refused while
  the role's view is in `[selected]`, for replace and append alike, `--force` does not lift it,
  and the error names the role override. It adds no membership test and no exemption: it calls
  `ItemsMixin._reject_unwritable_body`, which TASK-1069 ST6 turns into the shared gate. An
  operator has no declared view and is always writable.
- The seeded operator template counts as unwritten, so the first `operator body` needs no
  `--force` (`views.body_is_unwritten` against `ServiceCore.pristine_body`, the existing rule).

### ST1 — Stop sq check reporting prose in a role body as a missing seeded tag

<!-- sq:subtask:ST1:body -->
**Goal.** A role body holding prose in place of its seeded view tag is a supported state: `sq check`
reports nothing for it and exits 0. Operators already carry no seeded view, so the report never
fires for them; the test pins that.

**Approach**

- In `_seeded_view_issues` (`_services/_maintenance.py`), a missing seeded tag on a **role** whose
  body region holds prose (`prose_beside_missing_tag`) adds no problem. The decision keys off the
  roster type `ROSTER_ROLE`, the same constant the function already reads; no new predicate.
- The skill branch (both the shadowed-runbook and the plain message) is unchanged.
- A role whose region lost its tag and holds no prose keeps today's "restore it with view add,
  or disable it" report. An absent region keeps deferring to `_absent_body_region_issues`.
- `sq repair` already leaves a prose body alone; the test pins it rather than changing it.
- Rewrite the function's docstring and the inline comment to state the present rule in one or two
  sentences each.

tag-lost-no-prose row goes red. Restore each.

### ST2 — Add the body verb to roles and operators through one shared helper

<!-- sq:subtask:ST2:body -->
**Goal.** `sq role <slug> body` and `sq operator <slug> body` set the body with `-m`, `--file` and
`--append`, refuse to replace a written body without `--force`, and `show` prints the stored body
the same way for both kinds.

**Approach**

- One helper in `_cli/_common.py`, `register_body_verb`, beside TASK-1070's roster helpers,
  registers `body` on an addressed roster subgroup. It drives `Service.set_body(item_id,
  resolve_body(...), append=..., force=...)` exactly as `skill_body` does now and prints the same
  line. Replace `skill_body` in `_cli/_skill.py` with the helper, so the three verbs are one.
- Role addressing keeps `_require_id` (a bundled slug that is not active asks to activate first).
- The role refusal is whatever `set_body` raises through the TASK-1069 gate; the CLI adds no check
  of its own. An operator never hits it.
- `show` on both kinds: the header card, then the body as stored (`read_body`). A role with prose
  prints the prose in place of the catalog definition; an operator with no prose prints the
  template text it prints today. Align `operator_show` with `show_role` only where they differ in
  that order; no other render change.
- The first write over an operator's template body needs no `--force` (`body_is_unwritten`
  against `pristine_body`). The case of an operator renamed by `update --title` before the first
  body write follows the manager's ruling on FEAT-1054; its row is in the table either way.

**Tests**

- The task body's *body verb by shape* table, every row and column.
- Meta: `set_body` is the only service entry point the three `body` verbs call, and no
  `roster_body_view_name` call is added under `_cli/`.
- CLI: `operator add` then `operator <slug> body --file` then `operator <slug> show` prints the
  profile; a second `body -m` without `--force` exits 1 and leaves the file hash unchanged.

**Falsify.** Pass `force=True` unconditionally from the helper: the written-profile row without
`--force` goes red (no refusal). Skip `_require_id` on role:
the inactive-bundled-role row goes red. Make the operator body verb write through `--append` on a
replace: the read-back column goes red. Restore each.

**Acceptance**

- FEAT-1054 US1 bullets 4 to 6 and the two refusal bullets (operator template unwritten; role
  refused while its view is in `[selected]`, writable after it is dropped, no second membership
  test).
- The handoff lists every new command, message and exit code for the tech writer.

### ST4 — Offer registration and a short interview to an unknown human

<!-- sq:subtask:ST4:body -->
**Goal.** When no registered operator matches the person, the greeting skill asks whether to register
them before anything else, and on a yes registers them and records a short interview as their body.

**Approach**

- Edit section 1 of `templates/views/greeting_skill.md.j2`: when `sq list -t operator` has no match,
  ask first; a no leaves the squad unchanged.

  project, their expertise, what they come to do), then `sq operator <slug> body --file <path>`
  with the answers as prose. `operator add` takes no answers. No file under `squads/` is edited by
  hand.

  `operator add` then `body --file` sequence, and no `--profile` option.
