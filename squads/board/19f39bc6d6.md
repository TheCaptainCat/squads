Review findings get treated, not triaged away. A finding the reviewer raised and reproduced earns a real disposition regardless of its severity — fixed, or WontFix with reasoning that engages the evidence, or a named home in a later release. Severity orders the work; it does not decide whether the work happens. Do not leave a finding Open with no decision, and do not dismiss an info-tier finding on the strength of its summary line.
