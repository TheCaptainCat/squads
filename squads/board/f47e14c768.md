ADR audit remediation rule. The audit exists to find holes and deprecated content, not to tidy prose. Partly wrong: amend the decision, and track what changed in its comments — the comment trail is the audit record, so an amendment without one is incomplete. Completely wrong: retire it. Retirement means Superseded when a later decision replaced it (carry the supersedes ref), or Deprecated when it is simply wrong with nothing replacing it. Do not manufacture a supersedes edge for a clause-level overlap: that is an amendment at both ends, with the older narrowed in place and the two linked related.
