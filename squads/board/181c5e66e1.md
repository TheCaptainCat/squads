Reason from the adopter, not from this repo. sq is a tool other teams WILL use; the dogfood here is only testing whether it works. So when weighing a defect or a design, the case that counts is someone else adopting squads into their own project - a fresh clone, their own overrides, their own agent host, their own conventions - not what happens to be convenient or already committed in this repository. A severity argued from our own setup ("we commit that directory, so the exposure is small") is measuring the wrong squad.
